# KPop Demon Hunters 
## My Favorite Movie

My favorite movie is **KPop Demon Hunters**. It is an animated movie that combines *K-pop, action, and comedy*. I love this movie because of its unique plot, amazing animation, and their catchy songs.

---

### About the Movie

KPop Demon Hunters follows a K-pop girl group called **HUNTR/X**. They are famous singers who secretly work as demon hunters to protect the world from evil demons.

### Main Characters

- **Rumi** - The leader and main vocalist of HUNTR/X.
- **Mira** - A confident and strong member of the group.
- **Zoey** - The youngest and energetic member.
- **Jinu** - The leader of the Saja Boys.
